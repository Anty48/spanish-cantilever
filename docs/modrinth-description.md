# Spanish Cantilevers

**Spanish Cantilevers** is an add-on for **[Create: Pantographs & Wires](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-pantographs-and-wires)** (P&W) that brings authentic overhead line equipment, as built across the Spanish railway network, to Minecraft!

It adds realistic conventional-network cantilevers, Iberian lattice masts, rigid overhead conductor rail for tunnels and the iconic red Spanish pantograph. Everything is based on real Adif and Renfe equipment.

**Credits to MrJulsen** for creating such an amazing mod! Spanish Cantilevers couldn't have been developed without Pantographs & Wires.

## Key features

### Spanish cantilevers

- **Fully configurable:** modelled after real Spanish conventional-network cantilevers, with a built-in 3D preview window.
- **3 contact wire positions:** inner, middle and outer zigzag settings to keep pantograph wear even.
- **3 insulator types:** horizontal support, hanging support and vertical support.
- **Flexible widths:** track spans from 1.5 to 6.5 blocks.
- **Diagonal stays:** cantilevers 3 blocks wide or more can be hung with a working diagonal stay attached to the mast, using the P&W wire coil in *Support Wire* mode.
- **Double cantilevers for junctions:** right click a cantilever with another one to put two on the same mast, with opposite zigzag and staggered insulators. Each wire goes to the cantilever on the side you click, and the top face links both at once (one wire, two catenaries) for the span before the junction.
- **Hanging cantilevers:** a cantilever that hangs from a horizontal beam (cantilever or power line brackets, or any solid block), with the insulator on top of the beam or hanging under it, a diagonal or bent arm and an adjustable width.

![Contact wire positions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/Anty48/spanish-cantilever/images/modes.jpg)

### Spanish masts

- **4 mast types:** flat lattice, flat diagonal lattice, square lattice and H masts (retextures of the P&W masts).
- **Full integration:** these masts share tags with the P&W masts, so they can also rust into the P&W rusted masts.
- **High-speed (LAV) masts:** GIF (blue) and ADIF (green) versions of the 4 masts, the colours of the two Spanish high-speed infrastructure managers. They never rust.
- **Mast insulators:** top blocks for flat masts with one or two hanging insulators (grey, GIF and ADIF) for the P&W power wire. Double insulators carry two wires, like high-speed feeders.
- **Concrete column mast:** a thin round concrete mast with a base at the bottom, and a column top with an insulator on each side of a rod along the track: each power wire goes to the side you click.

### Rigid catenary & tunnel supports

- **Overhead conductor rail:** perfect for tunnels and underground lines. It is strung like a regular wire, curves smoothly through the supports and powers pantographs.
- **Tunnel supports:** wall and ceiling variants, chosen automatically from the surface you click.
- **Configurable sizes:** overall scaling (100%, 125%, 150%) of the support and the rigid profile.
- **Seamless transitions:** flexible catenary outside the tunnel portal connects directly to the rigid conductor rail inside.

![Rigid catenary in a tunnel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/Anty48/spanish-cantilever/images/tunnel_inside.jpg)

### Spanish pantograph

- The iconic **red-painted frame** seen on Spanish trains such as Renfe commuter and regional units (retexture of the P&W pantograph).

## Crafting & integration

- All blocks and items are crafted with **Spanish Steel** (iron ingots + black dye).
- Cantilevers use Create's **Sequenced Assembly**.
- Works with recipe viewers such as **JEI**.
