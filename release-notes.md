## 0.2.0 (Forge 1.20.1)

**High-speed (LAV) masts**
- New blue versions of the four Iberian masts (flat, flat diagonal, square and H-beam), in the blue of
  Spanish high-speed lines. They never weather, so there is no waxed version.
- New Blue Iberian Steel Ingot: 8 Iberian Steel Ingots around a blue dye make 8.

**Mast top insulators**
- New top blocks for flat lattice masts: a crossbar with one or two hanging insulators, in blue (LAV Mast
  Top Insulator, LAV Mast Top Double Insulator and their diagonal versions) and in grey (Iberian Mast Top
  Insulator...). On top of a flat mast they continue it, and a single insulator top faces the direction
  you are looking at.
- They take P&W's power wire. A double top sends both wires to a single attachment point, and two double
  tops connect wire to wire, like high-speed feeders.

**Fixes**
- The pantograph no longer sinks into the rigid tunnel catenary: it now touches the bottom of the profile.

Requires Create: Pantographs & Wires `1.20.1-beta-0.2.3-C6`, Create 6.0, DragonLib and GeckoLib.
P&W is in beta: back up your worlds.
