## 0.3.0 (NeoForge 1.21.1)

**ADIF and GIF high-speed masts**
- New green ADIF versions of the four Iberian masts (flat, flat diagonal, square and H-beam) and of the
  mast insulators. Like the blue ones, they never weather.
- The blue masts are now named after GIF (GIF Flat Lattice Mast...) and the green ones after ADIF, the two
  Spanish high-speed infrastructure managers.
- New ADIF Steel Ingot. GIF and ADIF Steel Ingots are now made directly from iron: 8 Iron Ingots around a
  blue or green dye make 8.
- The base ingot is now called Spanish Steel Ingot (same item and recipe).

**Concrete column mast**
- New Concrete Column Mast: 6 from smooth stone, stone and smooth stone in a column. The bottom block of a
  column has a base and the blocks above only continue the shaft, like P&W's concrete post. Cantilevers and
  brackets attach to it.
- New Concrete Column Mast Insulator for the top: a rod along the track with an insulator on each side.
  P&W's power wire goes to the end on the side you right click, so one wire runs along each side of the
  track. The rod points where you are looking when you place it.

**Other changes**
- "Top" removed from the names of the mast insulators (Iberian Mast Insulator, GIF LAV Mast Double
  Insulator...).
- Creative tab sorted: cantilever and tools, ingots, then the grey, GIF and ADIF families.

Requires Create: Pantographs & Wires `1.21.1-beta-0.2.3-C6`, Create 6.0, DragonLib and GeckoLib.
P&W is in beta: back up your worlds.
