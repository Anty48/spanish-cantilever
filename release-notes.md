## 0.1.0 (Forge 1.20.1)

First release of Spanish Cantilevers: configurable Spanish cantilevers with diagonal stays, Iberian
masts and Spanish mast parts, rigid overhead conductor rail with tunnel supports, and the red Spanish
pantograph.

Requires Create: Pantographs & Wires `1.20.1-beta-0.2.3-C6`, Create 6.0, DragonLib and GeckoLib.
P&W is in beta: back up your worlds.
