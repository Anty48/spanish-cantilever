## 0.4.0 (NeoForge 1.21.1)

**Hanging Spanish Cantilever**
- New cantilever that hangs from a horizontal beam, like the ones under the portal beams of Spanish
  stations. Place it from below on a Spanish or P&W Cantilever Bracket, on a Power Line Bracket, or under
  any solid block.
- The block sits right above the wire. The arm hangs further along the beam, on the far side from you, and
  a holder goes back to the contact wire.
- The insulator sits on top of the beam or hangs under it (default), at the right height for each beam.
- Settings window: insulator (horizontal or vertical, on top or hanging), arm shape (diagonal or bent),
  width from 1 to 3 (moves the arm away from the wire; the holder gets longer) and catenary height
  (measured from the underside of the beam). It also has the Set default config button.
- Crafted from a Spanish Cantilever, and back.

**Double cantilever for junctions**
- Right click a placed Spanish Cantilever with another one to add a second cantilever on the same mast,
  next to the first, on the side you click. The mast plate widens to hold both.
- The new one takes the opposite zigzag: an inner one gets an outer one, a middle or outer one gets an
  inner one. The outer one reaches a bit further out and the top insulators are staggered, so the two
  wires stay apart.
- The inner one of a pair uses the short diagonal of the other modes, with a longer holder that brings its
  contact wire right under its insulator.
- Width up to 2.5 each, and the top insulator always sits on top of the bar.
- Catenary height, Y offset and support wire are the same on both: changing them on one changes the other.
  Supporter heights can differ by up to two steps.
- Wires: with the P&W wire coil, click a side face of the block to link the cantilever on that side, or the
  top face to link both at once (one wire, two catenaries). Use the top face for the span before the
  junction, coming from a single cantilever.
- Right clicking with an empty hand opens the settings of the cantilever on the side you click. Breaking
  it gives back both cantilevers.

Requires Create: Pantographs & Wires `1.21.1-beta-0.2.3-C6`, Create 6.0, DragonLib and GeckoLib.
P&W is in beta: back up your worlds.
